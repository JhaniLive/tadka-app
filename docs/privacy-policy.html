Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your family details, kitchen list, recipes and plans are stored only on your phone.

What leaves your phone, and when

Only when you ask Tadka for something that needs AI (meal ideas, a recipe, a week or tiffin plan, a photo of your kitchen or a vegetable, or the chef chat), the app sends what that request needs to the Tadka server:

  • family members’ first names, ages, gender, spice level, allergies, health notes you added, and BMI if you entered height and weight;
  • your region, diet, kitchen equipment, kitchen items, and the language you chose;
  • the photo you chose, for photo features;
  • a random install ID, used only to apply fair daily limits.

The Tadka server (Cloudflare) passes the request to an AI model through OpenRouter and returns the answer. The Tadka server doesn’t store your requests or photos. OpenRouter and the model provider handle requests under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Camera / photos: only when you take or pick a food photo.
  • Microphone: only while hands-free cook mode is on, to hear commands like “next”. Speech is handled by your phone’s speech service.
  • Notifications: local reminders (use-today items, tiffin time, night-before prep), scheduled on your phone.

Health information

Health notes are general cooking guidance, not medical advice. Follow your doctor’s advice for any condition or allergy.

Deleting your data

Family → “Delete all my data” erases everything on the phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.
