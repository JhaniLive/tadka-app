Why Tadka exists

Most of us love good food, but nobody taught us to cook. We watch the same video again and again, and still the dal comes out different, because their family isn’t our family: their “2 cups” and “a little salt” are not ours.

Tadka knows who eats at your table: their ages, the spice each one likes, allergies, health and BMI. It works out exactly how much to cook, in grams, for your kitchen, so food tastes right and nothing goes to waste.

Everything a home cook needs

Built for Indian kitchens: pressure-cooker whistles, tadka, tiffins and leftovers.

Sized to the gram

Every recipe is scaled to your family’s real portions (kids, adults, elders), in grams, ounces, or cups and spoons.

One pot, everyone happy

Tadka finds the spice level for the whole table, then shows a small extra tadka for those who like it hot. Kids’ plates come out first.

Your region, or the best of India

Home style from your state, or the version a place is famous for: Hyderabadi bagara baingan, Andhra gutti vankaya, Bengali begun bhaja…

Cook mode

Big steps, the flame level, timers, a whistle counter, the amounts each step needs, read aloud. Hands-free: just say “next”.

Snap your kitchen

Photograph the vegetable basket or fridge. Tadka lists everything with how long it keeps, and plans meals that use it first.

What is this?

Snap any vegetable: its name in Indian languages, nutrition, how to pick, store and cut it, and the famous ways to cook it.

Tiffin planner

Lunch boxes for office and school that stay good till lunch, with a timed morning plan so you leave on time.

Week plan & shopping list

Seven days of meals around what you have, and a list of only what’s missing. Tick it off and it moves into your kitchen.

Leftover rescue

Leftover rice, rotis or dal become something new tomorrow. Cooked too much? Store it right or share it.

Health-aware

Allergy alerts checked in the app, nutrition per person, and gentle care for diabetes, BP, PCOS, pregnancy and more.

Fasting days, if you want

Choose a vrat, Ramzan sehri or iftar, Jain or your own rule, and every idea follows it. Never assumed.

Learns your taste

Too salty? Too much? Tell Tadka after cooking, and the next recipes adjust salt, spice and portions.

Install in a minute

  1. Download
    Tap “Download for Android” on your phone. The file is called Tadka.apk.
  2. Allow it once
    Android asks to allow installs from your browser. Allow it, then tap Install.
  3. Set up your table
    Add who eats at home and what’s in your kitchen. Then: what’s for dinner?

Honest notes

Recipes are written fresh for your family by AI from traditional home-cooking technique, not copied from any chef. Amounts, allergy checks and nutrition totals are worked out in the app; nutrition is an estimate. Health notes are general guidance, not medical advice. Your family and kitchen stay on your phone; read the privacy policy.
